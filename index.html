Biography

Badri Narayan Subudhi received the B.E. degree in electronics and communication engineering from Bijupatnaik University of Technology, Rourkela, India, and the M.Tech. degree in electronics system & communication from the National Institute of Technology, Rourkela, during 2008–2009. He received his PhD in 2014 under CSIR-SRF fellowship from the Machine Intelligence Unit, Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, India. He is currently an Associate Professor with the Indian Institute of Technology Jammu, India. Before this, he was an Assistant Professor at the National Institute of Technology Goa, India, from 2014 to 2017. His research interests include video processing, image processing, machine learning, pattern recognition, and remote sensing image analysis. He published 85+ papers in top-leading journals and conferences. He is an Associate Editor of IEEE Access, SN Computer Science, BMC Medical Imaging. He is the recipient of the Bose-Ramagnosi Award for the year 2010-11. He received DST/CSIR travel research grant in 2011. He was serving as the Governing body member for Asia Pacific Neural Network society for the year 2024-25.
